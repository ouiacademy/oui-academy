FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Completely free, no account needed. Just open the exam room and start.

Can I speak out loud, or do I have to type?

Both. On Chrome, Edge (desktop, Android) and Safari (iPhone, Mac) you tap the mic and speak French — it is transcribed and sent to the examiner, who answers out loud in French. If your browser has no speech support (e.g. Firefox), you can still type, like a normal chat.

Why 3 min 30 and not 3 minutes?

In the official TCF Canada format, Tâche 2 lasts 5 min 30: 2 minutes of preparation and 3 min 30 of interaction. The room defaults to that, but you can choose 3 minutes (or 5 minutes for relaxed practice) before drawing a sujet.

Does the clock run while the AI is thinking?

No. The clock pauses while waiting for the AI examiner's reply, so your 3 min 30 is real speaking time — like sitting in front of a human examiner who answers instantly.

How accurate is the feedback and estimated level?

Feedback follows the Tâche 2 criteria (task completion, variety of questions, vocabulary, grammar, register, reactivity). It is a practice tool and only indicative — the AI can be wrong, and speech-to-text hides pronunciation errors. For a score close to the real thing, book a session with Hải.

Is my conversation stored?

No. It is only sent to the server so the AI examiner can reply and evaluate, and is not saved in any database. Close the page and it's gone.

Ready to draw your sujet?

Each round takes about 6 minutes. Do one a day and within two weeks you’ll be asking questions much faster and more naturally.

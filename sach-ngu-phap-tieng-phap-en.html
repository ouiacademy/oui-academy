Sample entry
A1

Gender and number of nouns

Le genre et le nombre des noms
Usage

In French, every noun has a gender: masculine or feminine — unlike Vietnamese, which doesn't mark gender. To make a noun plural, you usually add -s at the end (this -s is silent).

Examples
un étudiant / une étudiantea male student / a female student
le livre → les livresa book → books
le journal → les journauxa newspaper → newspapers

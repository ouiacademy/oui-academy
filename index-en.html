FAQ

Still have questions before you start?

Online or in person in Paris?

Both — you learn on your own schedule, whether that's online or in person in Paris.

What's the difference between the Speaking and Complete Curriculum tracks?

The Speaking Curriculum focuses specifically on Speaking & Writing exam skills, with 60–75 minute sessions. The Complete Curriculum covers all 4 skills following the Édito (Didier FLE) textbook, with 3-hour sessions — better suited to learners building a foundation from scratch.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is per session or per hour depending on the track — see the Speaking Curriculum and Complete Curriculum pricing pages for details. Paying for a full level gets you 10% off, and 3-installment plans are available.

Are 1:1 private lessons available?

Yes — both tracks offer a 1:1 private option, with pricing on the corresponding track's pricing page.

Who teaches the classes?

Hải — who has passed DALF C2 three times with a perfect 50/50 Listening-Speaking score, and has taught DELF B2 at Sorbonne Nouvelle University, Paris. See his full background in the Teacher section.

Is this for every level, or just beginners?

Every level — Oui Academy takes students from A0 (complete beginner) through C2, matched to your DELF, DALF, or TCF Canada goal.

How do I get started?

Book a free consultation and we'll figure out the right track and level together.

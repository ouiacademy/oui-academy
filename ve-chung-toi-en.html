About

Oui Academy

Oui Academy is an online French-language training organization specializing in exam preparation for DELF, DALF, and TCF Canada, with a focus on the Speaking and Writing skills.[1]

Overview

Oui Academy accompanies students from their first lessons through international certification exams, with a curriculum personalized to each student's level and goals.[1] As of 2026, the organization has taught more than 300 students based in Vietnam, Canada, the United Kingdom, the United States, Mali, and Guinea, across all levels from A0 to C2.[1] Instruction is delivered online or in person in Paris, France.[1]

Programs

Oui Academy runs two main tracks:

  • Speaking Track (Lộ trình Nói) — level-based speaking sessions from A1 to C2, covering grammar, phonetics, and the topics of each exam's oral component.[2]
  • Comprehensive Track (Lộ trình Toàn Diện) — all four language skills, based on the Édito textbook series (Didier FLE publishing), levels A1 through C1, taught in 3-hour weekly sessions.[3]

The three exams the curriculum targets are DELF (levels A1–B2), DALF (levels C1–C2), and TCF Canada, the latter used for Canadian immigration and study-permit applications.[1] The organization also offers a free online level-placement test[4] and an interactive French sound chart for pronunciation practice with recording playback comparison.[5]

Teaching team

The lead instructor is Hải, who has passed the DALF C2 exam three times, including a perfect 50/50 score on the Listening-Speaking component in 2026 and a perfect 25/25 on the Speaking component of the DALF C1 in 2022.[6] He has taught DELF B2 at Sorbonne Nouvelle University in Paris, holds a university teaching pedagogy certification from Hanoi National University of Education, is currently pursuing a master's degree in Teaching French as a Foreign Language (FLE) in France, and has spoken at the Campus France forum in 2020 and 2023.[6] He is an alumnus of the French-English bilingual program at Chu Văn An High School for the Gifted in Hanoi.[6]

Referral program

Oui Academy runs a student referral program: both the referrer and the referred student each receive one free lesson once the referred student books a trial lesson and enrolls in a track.[7]
